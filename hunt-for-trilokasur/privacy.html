Hunt for Trilokasur – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how Hunt for Trilokasur (the "Game"), published by Vahdam Games ("we", "us"), handles information.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

None. The Game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any account. It has no sign-in, no chat, no advertisements and no in-app purchases. We run no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service, and the Game does not read your device's advertising ID.

2. INFORMATION SAVED ON YOUR DEVICE

Your game progress (unlocked levels) and settings (sound, controls, layout) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes them.

3. THIRD PARTIES

The Game is made with the Unity game engine. We have switched off Unity's analytics, crash-reporting and performance-reporting services, and the Game includes no advertising or tracking software. Google Play, from which you install the Game, handles its own data under Google's privacy policy.

4. CHILDREN

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you write to us by email, we use your message and email address only to reply, and we delete them when the matter is closed.

6. SECURITY

The Game does not transmit your progress or settings anywhere.

7. CHANGES

We may update this policy. If a future update adds advertising or any other data collection, we will update this policy before that update is released and tell you in the Game. The current version is always available in the Game (Settings > Privacy) and on this page.
