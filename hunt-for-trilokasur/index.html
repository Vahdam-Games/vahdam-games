Hunt for Trilokasur

A 2D pixel-art action platformer inspired by the Puranas. As Chitrasur, descend through the seven gates of the lower loks to stop Trilokasur.

Coming soon on Google Play

The game

  • Sword, bow, shield and dash, with powers granted by the Devs as you progress
  • Seven gates, each with its own lok, enemies, hazards and a commander to defeat
  • Touch controls you can rearrange, plus Xbox and PlayStation controller support
  • Free to play: no ads, no purchases, no account, no sign-in
  • For players aged 13 and over
